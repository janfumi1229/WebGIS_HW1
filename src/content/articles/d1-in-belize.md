Excited to embark on a new chapter in Belize! 🇧🇿✈️🇹🇼

As part of the Taiwanese Pathfinders Program, I have officially arrived in Belize alongside five fellow youth delegates to begin an intensive month-long overseas internship.

Over the coming weeks, I will be working closely with the Orange Walk Town Council,  focusing on local municipal governance, community engagement, and environmental and solid waste management initiatives. Stepping into the vibrant community of Orange Walk (known as sugar village) offers a unique vantage point to understand how municipal leadership navigates resource circulation, environmental education, and public administration on the ground.

A heartfelt thank you to Ambassador Chi and the Embassy of Taiwan in Belize for the warm welcome dinner upon our arrival. His encouragement to step beyond comfort zones, listen deeply to local communities, and build meaningful partnerships resonates strongly as we kick off our projects.

Looking forward to collaborating with local mentors and the volunteers from JICA (Japanese International Cooperation Association), learning from the resilient community in Orange Walk, and contributing to strengthening the bridges of youth diplomacy and sustainable development between Taiwan and Belize.

Let the journey begin!
